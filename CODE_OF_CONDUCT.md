# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

CryFox is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and safe environment for everyone. We value privacy, security, and respect for our users and contributors.

## Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn
- Focus on what is best for the community and users
- Show empathy towards other community members
- Be transparent and honest

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Violence, threats of violence, or encouraging violence

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, please report it by contacting us. All reports will be handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Violations of this code of conduct may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project, at the discretion of the maintainers.
